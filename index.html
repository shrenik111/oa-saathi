OA-SATHI

AI-assisted OA risk screening · NER

Early screening for joint health

This tool helps healthcare workers record a patient's symptoms and combine them with gait, wearable-sensor, or imaging data collected offline to generate a preliminary osteoarthritis (OA) risk score. It does not replace a doctor's diagnosis.
